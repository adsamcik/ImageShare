ImageShare helps you prepare photos before you send them. Resize, compress, convert, and strip metadata on your Android device without cloud uploads, accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Key features:

• Fast presets for common sharing needs
• Batch processing for multiple images
• Target file size mode for upload limits
• Custom dimensions with aspect-ratio lock
• Long-edge, percentage, exact-size, and original-size options
• Metadata stripping for GPS, camera serial numbers, maker notes, and other EXIF fields
• Modern Material 3 interface for Android 10 and newer

Privacy-first by design:

• No internet permission
• No analytics or telemetry
• No ads
• No broad photo-library permission
• Originals remain in their original location
• Processed copies remain in app-private cache until you save or share them

ImageShare supports JPEG, PNG, and WebP output.

ImageShare is open source under the GNU GPL v3 license:
https://github.com/adsamcik/ImageShare

Choose a photo, pick a preset, process locally, and share with confidence.
